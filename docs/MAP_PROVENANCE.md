# ArcGIS figure provenance

## Existing native paper figures

The images are copies of Figure_3_ArcGIS.png and Figure_4_ArcGIS.png from the original project's Paper/ArcGIS_Figures folder. The original map note identifies them as ArcGIS Pro 3.6 native exports at 300 dpi. They were reused here without cropping away legends or basemap attribution. No new analysis, model run or manuscript publication is implied.

The source project is Homelessness_Paper_Maps.aprx. The recorded map audit checks all 141 keyed polygon values and PIT point counts, agreement of centroid coordinates with the notebook export, no overlap between background and study CoCs, no broken layers and no overflowing legends. Those are existing recorded checks, not a new full scientific reproduction.

Both images use the same five graduated homelessness count classes. Housing colors refer to the 2024 affordability index; veteran colors refer to 2022 ACS civilian veteran population. The outcome circles refer to 2021 PIT counts. Gray regions are outside the sample. Yellow marker coordinates are the analysis polygon centroids transformed to WGS84 for display. These are not locations of individual people.

The ArcGIS and basemap source attribution remains visible in each image. The original APRX, geodatabase and research inputs remain local; no unpublished manuscript was uploaded.
